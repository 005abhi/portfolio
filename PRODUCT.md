# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, potential collaborators, and fellow developers who land on the portfolio while evaluating Abhinandan. They're on the site for 30–120 seconds deciding whether to reach out. They want to feel like they've met someone interesting, not just scanned a resume.

## Product Purpose

A personal brand site for Abhinandan Patro — Full Stack Developer based in India. The site exists to build presence and credibility: leave every visitor with a clear sense of who Abhinandan is, what he builds, and why he's worth remembering. Success is a follow, a DM, or an email — not just a pageview.

## Brand Personality

Playful, precise, alive. The energy of a developer who genuinely enjoys the craft — unexpected details, motion that earns its place, a site that feels handmade rather than generated.

## Anti-references

- The generic dark-navy dev portfolio (skills grid, timeline, contact form, every section identical)
- Startup SaaS landing pages (gradient hero metrics, glassmorphism cards, product-pitch energy)
- Over-engineered 3D scenes that load forever and get in the way of the work

## Design Principles

1. **Personality over polish** — a surprising interaction beats a flawless grid. The site should feel like it has opinions.
2. **Motion earns its place** — every animation should reveal something or delight the eye; no animation for animation's sake.
3. **Show the work** — let projects and experience carry weight; the design frames them, doesn't compete with them.
4. **One of a kind** — no design pattern should be guessable from the category alone. If it looks like "a developer portfolio," push further.
5. **Fast by default** — performance is part of the craft. Heavy motion should degrade gracefully; content never gates on a transition.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as a baseline — readable contrast, keyboard navigation that works, no content gated behind animation. Reduced motion respected.
